How fees work

No hidden charges. Ever.

ReLoop earns from two clearly labelled amounts, and both are always shown before you pay:

  • 1
    Customer platform fee
    A small percentage added on top of the product price or repair amount. You see it at checkout and on every quote.
  • 2
    Provider commission
    A percentage deducted from what the seller or repairer earns. Providers see their gross, commission and net for every sale.
  • 3
    ReLoop total commission
    The sum of the two. We show it too, so everyone knows exactly what the platform keeps.

Payments in this build are simulated. No real money is transferred and no card or bank details are collected.
